PRIVACY
on-device!

Short version: Subway Movers watches you through your webcam to read your movements, and none of that ever leaves your machine. Here's the detail.

Your camera stays yours
Pose tracking runs entirely on your own device. Your camera frames are processed locally, in real time, and are never uploaded, recorded, saved to disk, or sent to any server. When you close the app, they're gone.
No accounts, no tracking
There is no sign-up and no login. Subway Movers does not collect personal information, does not use analytics or advertising trackers, and does not build a profile on you.
What leaves your device
Only ordinary web requests needed to load the page and its fonts (Google Fonts). The pose-detection model runs locally. If you launch a third-party game from the app, that game has its own privacy policy that Subway Movers does not control.
Open source
Don't take our word for it — the whole thing is on GitHub. Read the code, check exactly what it does, and open an issue if anything looks off.
